## Amendment 1 — surface gate

The surface rule is settled, not an OPEN threshold: surface/bg contrast >=1.2:1 for every theme, minimum HSL lightness movement from that theme’s bg, lighter for dark and darker for light. Do not merely lighten the existing surface or change hue/saturation. A deterministic computation and its minimal-step assertion must accompany the per-theme hex report; no hand-picked palette values. Check text contrast on the resulting surfaces, including selected/disabled/hint roles, rather than retaining old ratios measured on old surfaces.
