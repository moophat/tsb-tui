# Fix 261113-65 round-3b — Qodo finding 5: lock refresh on logged-out new/attach

**Branch:** feat/drive-send-gate-screen-lock | **PR:** #65 | **Head:** 294fa2d
**Model:** strata/strata

## Finding

`Commands.run` calls `_refresh_locks()` for `new` and `attach` before `op_new`/`op_attach`
check that drive is logged in, so a new or attach while logged out sends GET /auth/me on
the unauthenticated client. Its 401 reaches `notify_api_error` and raises a "Request failed"
toast although the command answers `not_logged_in`.

## Fix

Guard `_refresh_locks()` with `self.d.screen() is not None` so the lock settings are
refreshed only when drive is logged in. The command's answer is unchanged.

**File changed:** `tsb_tui/drive/commands.py` (line 372)
